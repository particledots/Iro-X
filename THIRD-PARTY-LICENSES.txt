1. Iroi firmware (third_party/iroi-firmware)
   Befaco, https://github.com/Befaco/Iroi, GNU GPL version 3 (see third_party/iroi-firmware/LICENSE).
   Imported at the commit named in third_party/iroi-firmware/SOURCE.txt, UNMODIFIED in Milestone 0. Every later change
   will be marked "Iro-x change" in the file and listed in third_party/iroi-firmware/IROX-CHANGES.md.
   Iro-x is an unofficial port. It is not made, approved or supported by Befaco. The Befaco panel design and artwork
   are not used. The Iroi README names Oneiroi (Roberto Noris, https://github.com/hirnlego) as its parent design.
   Still to be checked in Milestone 1 (not yet verified, so not claimed here): the Iroi source includes headers from
   Rebel Technology's OpenWare platform (GPL) and code adapted from Mutable Instruments (stmlib, Plaits, Rings).
   Neither is in Befaco's repository. Their licences and notices will be listed here once they are brought in.
